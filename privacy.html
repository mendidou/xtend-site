Privacy

What Xtend does and doesn't collect.

Short version: nothing leaves your devices. Long version below.

At a glance
  • No personal data collected, stored, or transmitted to our servers.
  • No accounts. No telemetry. No analytics. No advertising. No tracking.
  • iPhone ⇄ Mac communication is direct over your Wi-Fi (Local tier).
  • GDPR & CCPA compliant by design (no data to access or delete).
  • Permissions are optional or scoped to the feature that needs them.

Data we collect

None. Xtend does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports to our servers. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no tracking.

How Xtend works on your network

The Local tier is a peer-to-peer connection between the iOS app and the macOS companion over your Wi-Fi. The simulator stream, touch events, build commands, and terminal data flow directly between your iPhone and your Mac on your local network. Nothing is routed through a third party.

Permissions Xtend asks for

  • Local Network (iOS): required to discover and connect to your Mac via Bonjour.
  • Microphone (iOS, optional): only used if you enable voice-to-text input in the terminal. Audio is sent to Apple's SFSpeechRecognizer for transcription, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Xtend does not record, store, or forward your audio anywhere else.
  • Speech Recognition (iOS, optional): same as above; required by Apple to enable speech-to-text input.
  • Screen Recording (macOS): required so the Mac companion can capture the iOS Simulator window for streaming. Granted via System Settings; revocable at any time.
  • Accessibility (macOS): required to forward touch and keyboard events from your iPhone into the simulator.

Third-party services

None in the Local tier. No analytics SDKs, no crash reporting services, no advertising networks. The future Remote tier (not yet shipping) will use encrypted WebRTC peer-to-peer between your iPhone and Mac, with a TURN relay used only as a fallback when direct P2P cannot be established. Details and a separate addendum will be published before that feature ships.

Data retention & deletion

Because Xtend does not collect or store any personal data on our servers, there is nothing to retain and nothing to delete. The only data Xtend handles — your simulator stream, terminal sessions, and build output — flows live between your iPhone and your Mac and is never written to remote storage. When the connection ends, the data is gone.

Your privacy rights (GDPR, CCPA & others)

Xtend is designed to be compliant with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) by collecting no personal data at all. The rights granted by these laws — access, correction, deletion, portability, and opt-out of sale — are trivially satisfied: there is no data on file to access, correct, delete, port, or sell.

How to exercise your rights: if you believe Xtend is processing data about you, email Maouizerat@gmail.com with the subject "Data Rights Request" and a description of what you'd like to access, correct, port, delete, or restrict. We will respond within 30 days. Because no personal data is collected, the response will typically be a confirmation that no records exist.

Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6): Xtend does not collect personal data, so no legal basis for processing is required. If a future feature changes this, the legal basis will be disclosed before that feature ships.

"Do Not Track": Xtend does not use tracking technologies regardless of DNT signal. The website honors DNT by default since it has no tracking to enable.

International transfers: No data leaves your devices in the Local tier. The optional iOS speech-to-text feature uses Apple's SFSpeechRecognizer, which may transmit short audio snippets to Apple's servers, located in regions covered by Apple's own data-transfer agreements (including SCCs for EU users).

Security

The Local tier relies on the security of your home or office Wi-Fi network. Communication between the iPhone and Mac stays on your local segment; we recommend running Xtend on networks with WPA2 or WPA3 encryption. No credentials are stored on remote servers because there are no remote servers in the Local tier. The future Remote tier will use end-to-end encryption between paired devices; that addendum will detail the cryptographic design before launch.

Cookies & tracking technologies

Xtend's iOS and macOS apps do not use cookies, web beacons, fingerprinting, or any tracking technology. This website uses no analytics or tracking either.

Children's privacy

Xtend is a developer tool intended for users 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes affecting how data is handled will be flagged in the iOS app's release notes.
